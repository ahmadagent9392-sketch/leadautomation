# Reviewing outreach

Score each 0 / 1 / 2:
1. Specific — names a dated, real observation about them.
2. True — every fact matches verified evidence; guesses are hedged.
3. Relevant — the offer clearly solves the observed problem.
4. Short — within the channel word limit; no filler.
5. Tone — about them, not about Ahmad; no hype, no buzzwords.
6. Next step — small and easy (video, mini audit, yes/no question).
7. Compliance — email footer present, no banned phrases, contact not blocked, right channel.

APPROVE_FOR_HUMAN: every score ≥ 1 and total ≥ 11.
REWRITE: give exact fixes (max 2 rounds).
REJECT: wrong person, weak proof, or offer doesn't fit.
