# Writing outreach

## Structure (all channels)
OBSERVATION → EVIDENCE → IMPACT → OFFER → SMALL NEXT STEP

## Per channel
- **email** (≤100 words, subject ≤ 4 words, lowercase ok, plain text, footer from policy.yaml)
- **linkedin_message** (≤70 words, no link in first message, Ahmad sends by hand)
- **upwork_proposal** (≤180 words: first line repeats their exact need; 2-3 steps of how you'd do it; one proof link;
  one question about their setup)
- **agency_pitch** (≤120 words: "your clients ask for X; I build it white-label; example; small paid test project")
- **referral_ask** (≤80 words, to a past client/contact: who exactly you help, ask for one intro)

## Good example (email)
Subject: your data entry role
"Hi Sara, I saw your Sept 20 post for a part-time assistant to copy Shopify orders into QuickBooks.
That's often 5-10 hours a week. I build small automations that do exactly this sync, usually in 5 days, for a fixed $400.
Want me to send a 2-minute video showing how it would work for your store?
— Ahmad"

## Bad (never)
"Hi, I help businesses leverage AI to revolutionize their workflows. Can we hop on a quick call?"

## Follow-ups
Each adds something new: a new fact, a mini teardown, a similar example, or a polite last note.
Never "just bumping this".
